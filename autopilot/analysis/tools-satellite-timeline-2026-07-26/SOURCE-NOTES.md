# Source Notes

## Decision question

Estimate the time required to reach a sustainable Tools operation and a vertically supplied Satellite operation, then identify achievements that may provide near-term capital.

## Metric definitions

- `Funding days`: capital gap divided by estimated hourly cash generation after bond interest.
- `Build days`: critical-path construction time, assuming independent buildings can be built in parallel.
- `Total time`: funding time plus critical-path build time.
- `Sustainable`: enough upstream capacity to supply the modeled retail building continuously at the target level.
- `Salvage credit`: an optimistic material-match estimate for dismantling coffee-specific buildings; it is not guaranteed cash.
- `Utilization`: the share of theoretical coffee operating profit actually realized over time.

## Report structure

1. Executive summary
2. Tools timeline and decision
3. Satellite timeline and decision
4. Achievement capital opportunities
5. Recommendations
6. Further questions
7. Caveats and sources

## Chart map

- Tools scenario comparison: horizontal bars; category = scenario, value = total days; takeaway = a sustainable Tools pivot is measured in weeks, not days.
- Satellite route comparison: horizontal bars; category = route, value = total months; takeaway = staged Tools reinvestment dominates saving directly from Coffee.
- Achievement audit: table; fields = observed metric, threshold, maximum possibly unclaimed reward, evidence strength, claim status.

## Source authority and conflicts

- Company cash, debt, buildings, queues, and completed actions use the latest local autopilot state and logs.
- Product economics and build costs use the current raw retail-model snapshot and checked game-facts.
- Achievement thresholds and rewards use the official Sim Companies realm guide.
- Authenticated achievement claim status could not be read because this execution sandbox cannot connect to the host Chrome CDP endpoint. Eligibility and claim status are therefore reported separately.
- Market prices, administration overhead, sale cadence, and material recovery can change. Long-horizon Satellite projections are decision estimates, not forecasts.

## Validation checks

- Recomputed post-upgrade cash, hourly bond interest, capital gaps, funding times, and critical paths from source fields.
- Compared optimistic and conservative material-recovery cases.
- Compared 60%, 70%, and 100% utilization cases.
- Rejected technically reachable minimum configurations when modeled operating profit was below the current Coffee baseline.
