been persisted. It records historical observations, not current inventory, prices, cash, offer
status, or standing permission to transact. Match the exact offer text and use fresh state for
every decision. Community claims belong in `pa-quests-guide.md` and remain unverified until the
game shows the result.

## Evidence rules

- An observed outcome applies only to a matching offer and choice. It does not prove current terms,
  current profitability, or universal quest behavior.
- A promised number is a ceiling until measured. Price requested goods at their current best use,
  including chain value and production opportunity cost.
- Do not automatically buy or produce missing goods. Compare the branch with current cash reserve,
  inventory, Transport, building time, debt, and competing plans.
- Insufficient-goods responses preserved two historical offers, but that is not a general parking
  mechanism.
- After a response, verify cash, relevant inventory, building changes, visible result text, and
  whether the choices disappeared. Append only the concise new observation; do not add a temporary
  production plan or current-company narrative to this file.
