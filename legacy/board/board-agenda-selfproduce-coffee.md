# AGENDA (owner-submitted): remove the Slaughterhouse, borrow, and build self-water + self-power to FULLY self-produce coffee and cut its cost?

**Submitted by the chairman (Jimmy) for the 21:00 board. Deliberate it on MEASURED numbers (skeptic
rule / Rule 4d). The chairman's GOAL is right — lower coffee cost — but test whether THIS is the way.**

## The proposal (four moves)
1. **Scrap the Slaughterhouse** to free a slot (scrap returns 100% of materials at company Lv.5).
2. **Take a bond/loan** to fund the build-out.
3. **Build a Water reservoir + a Power plant** to self-supply the coffee chain's inputs.
4. Result: "fully self-produced coffee" at a lower unit cost.

## The MEASURED numbers the board must weigh (computed 2026-07-24 off game-facts + live ticker)
- **The chain is REAL and vertical (owner is right):** power → water(0.2 power/water) →
  seeds(0.1 water/seed) → beans(0.5 water + 1 seed) → powder(10 beans). Power IS embedded, via water.
- **Per powder the FULL chain carries 6 water (5 in beans + 1 in seeds) and 1.2 power.**
- **Self-supplying the whole chain saves:** water self-cost $0.146 (plant wage + 0.2 power) vs buy
  $0.383 → 6 water × $0.237 = **$1.42 saved per powder = 3.7%** of the $38.25 price.
- **At 2 Mills (~36 powder/h): total saving ≈ $51/h.**

## The COST of the four moves (why the arithmetic says NO)
- **Slot opportunity cost is the killer:** the SAME 2 slots built as **two more Mills** add ~36
  powder/h of OUTPUT ≈ **$2,000/h**, versus self-supplying the chain which only cuts input cost by
  **~$51/h**. **Building more output beats in-sourcing cheap inputs by ~39×.** A Water reservoir /
  Power plant makes coffee's *inputs*, not coffee — a scarce slot is worth ~$1,000/h making the powder
  itself, not ~$25/h shaving the price of a $0.38 input.
- **Removing the Slaughterhouse throws away $324/h MEASURED** (this board's own number) to free a slot
  — and the water/power that replaces it earns LESS ($73–224/h) than the steak it displaces. Net loss.
- **Debt:** borrowing to fund a negative-ROC move (save $46/h against a $2,000/h slot cost) is exactly
  what the bonds agenda forbids — never borrow against a low / bookStale return.

## The skeptic frame (this is a REPEAT pattern, name it)
This is structurally the **fourth appearance of the vertical-integration trap** the board already killed
three times on aerospace/HGEC: *self-producing an input lowers the unit cost but consumes a scarce slot,
and the OUTPUT VOLUME does not increase — you make the same powder, $1.27 cheaper, using slots worth 40×
the saving.* Cheaper input ≠ more profit when slots, not input cost, are the binding constraint.

## The right way to "lower coffee cost" (for the board to consider instead)
- Keep buying water (deep book, $0.38, non-blocking) — it is 7% of powder cost and trivial to source.
- Keep the Slaughterhouse ($324/h) unless a slot use beats it — water/power do not.
- Put scarce slots on the HIGHEST realizable $/h (powder itself, then the endgame lines), not on
  self-supplying a $0.38 input. Coffee cost falls fastest by SCALING powder and its market, not by
  in-sourcing cheap water.

A yes/no/amend on each of the four moves, each decided by the number that kills or saves it. If any part
survives the skeptic check on MEASURED numbers, execute it; if it is the HGEC trap again, kill it and say

**Chairman's steer:** I want cheaper coffee. Find the cheapest path on MEASURED numbers — and if
