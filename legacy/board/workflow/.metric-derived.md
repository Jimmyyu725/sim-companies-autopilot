## What was built

A **Leontief backward demand-propagation** over the recipe graph:

```
finalDemand(X)   = unitsSoldAnHour(X)                          # terminal RETAIL absorption /hr
derivedDemand(X) = Σ over every product Y whose recipe uses X: totalDemand(Y) × recipe(Y)[X]
totalDemand(X)   = finalDemand(X) + derivedDemand(X)           # gross realm throughput /hr
```

i.e. solve `x = f + Aᵀx` where `A[Y][X]` = units of X per unit of Y. The recipe graph is an
**acyclic DAG** (verified: 0 cycles), so fixed-point iteration converges in 9 steps. This is exactly
the chairman's hypothesis: a product's B2B demand ≈ sum over downstream consumers, each weighted by
its OWN realizable demand, recursively. **game-facts recipes fully support this** — every resource
carries `recipe{input:qty}`, and the reverse graph is trivially derivable.

## KEY TEST — does it rank crude/dough HIGH and aerospace/flight-computer ~ZERO?

**YES, directionally — with one mandatory data correction.** Verdict is nuanced, so read the two
caveats; they are load-bearing.

### The metric RESCUES crude and dough (the win)
Both have `consumption = 0` AND `unitsSoldAnHour = 0` — the exact trap that makes consumption-only or
retail-only rankers score them ZERO and KILL them. Derived-demand instead inherits their downstream
pull and scores them clearly non-zero:
- **crude-oil** → refined into **diesel (retail 105/hr) + petrol (retail 103/hr)** + plastics + carbon
  chain. Real, deep, high-volume. Ranks #14, top ~9%. **NOT killed.** ✓
- **dough** → **pizza (retail 10/hr) + apple-pie (retail 10/hr)** + bread. Real B2B, non-zero, rank 51.
  ✓ **Data correction:** in game-facts dough (k137) is consumed by bread/apple-pie/pizza — NOT by
  samosa. Samosa (k143) uses **flour (k133)**, not dough. Dough's B2B is real but via pizza/pie, not
  catering. (Don't repeat the "samosa eats dough" assumption; it's false in the current facts.)

### The metric SINKS the pure-aerospace parts (the other win)
cockpit, attitude-control, rocket-engine, jet-engine inherit demand ONLY from planes/rockets/satellites,
whose **entire realm retail basket is ~7 units/hr combined** across every aircraft+rocket+satellite. Trace:
`rocket-engine ← orbital-booster(×34) & starship(×7) ← BFR (sells ~1/hr)`. The terminal-demand ceiling
is tiny, so the parts stay near the bottom (ranks 45–67), while the buggy paper-$/h ranker crowns them
at $2,600–3,849/bldg-hr. Derived-demand correctly demotes them. ✓

### CAVEAT 1 — the `unitsSoldAnHour = 1` floor is a data trap that inflates everything upstream
game-facts floors ultra-luxury terminals (BFR, jumbojet, private-jet, satellite, sub-orbital rocket) at
`unitsSold = 1`, but the chairman **live-measured aerospace realm demand at ~0.057/hr**. That coarse
"1/hr" floor, multiplied by giant recipe coefficients (fuselage ×40, rocket-engine ×34), leaks upstream.
Re-seeding final demand with the measured **0.057/hr** collapses the mirage:

| Product | derived @ unitsSold=1 floor | derived @ measured 0.057/hr terminal |
|---|---:|---:|
| cockpit (k81) | 6 | **0.34** |
| attitude-control (k82) | 8 | **0.46** |
| rocket-engine (k86) | 41 | **2.34** |
| jet-engine (k89) | 6 | **0.34** |
| combustion-engine (k52) | 10 | **9.06** ← stays (also feeds real cars/trucks) |
| **crude-oil (k10)** | **4099** | **407** ← durable core; the other 3,690 was aerospace artifact |
| dough (k137) | 30 | **30** (unchanged — real pizza/pie terminals) |
| steak / planks / apples | 35 / 140 / 322 | unchanged (real high-volume terminals) |

Two lessons: (a) all *pure*-aerospace parts fall to **0.3–2.3/hr = effectively DEAD**, confirming the
MIRAGE flag. (b) **combustion-engine stays ~9/hr** because it also feeds economy-car/truck/luxury-car
(real retail) — the metric correctly refuses to kill a genuine cross-sector engine. Good behavior.

### CAVEAT 2 — recipe-multiplier UNIT inflation distorts absolute magnitudes
Raw units/hr over-weights deep, high-coefficient chains. **94% of crude's face-value 4,099 is a single
carbon-fiber → carbon-composite (×8) → fuselage (×40) → aerospace (×34–40) chain** dead-ending in the
same unitsSold=1 luxury terminals. Crude's *economically real* durable demand is only the diesel+petrol+
plastics core (~156–407/hr). At face value rocket-engine (41) even edges out dough (30) purely from a
34× coefficient. **Fix:** seed final demand from **measured retail (market-snapshots), not the coarse
floor**, and compare $-weighted (or against the terminal-demand ceiling) — not raw unit counts across
products of wildly different unit value.

## Edge cases — derived-demand must be COMBINED with retail, NEVER replace it

- **Leaf consumer goods score ZERO on derived and would be wrongly killed.** samosa (k143) is a pure
  graph LEAF (nothing consumes it) → derivedDemand = **0**. Yet market-snapshots shows samosa
  **demand 0.95, saturation 0.10** = the single best draining-lake volume king (~380k/day). Steak is
  similar: derived 0, but retail 35/hr real. **A derived-only ranker kills the best product.** Use
  `totalDemand = retail + derived`, always.
- **Retail-real but oversupplied lakes.** apples: totalDem 322 (derived 212 + retail 110) looks great,
  but snapshot **saturation 1.49** = crowded, price-suppressed lake. Derived-demand says "demand exists";
  it does NOT say "the lake is drainable." Cross-check saturation before scaling into it.
- **`unitsSoldAnHour` and `consumption` fields are both unreliable seeds** (0 for crude/dough/samosa
  which are all real). Seed from measured market demand; use recipes for the B2B propagation.

## Bottom line for the ranker (`realizable.js`)

1. Recipe-graph derived-demand is **computable and correct in shape** — it rescues crude & dough from
   the kill-list and demotes pure-aerospace to near-zero. The seed hypothesis **holds**.
2. It is **not sufficient alone**: seed final demand from **measured retail** (not the unitsSold=1 floor),
   report **`totalDemand = measuredRetail + derived`**, and gate with **saturation** (drainability) and
   ideally **$-weighting** (to defuse recipe-multiplier unit inflation).
3. Composite realizable score sketch: `realizable(X) ≈ (measuredRetail(X) + derivedDemand(X)) × price(X) ×
   drainability(X)` where drainability falls as saturation rises. This scores samosa/construction/crude
   HIGH, apples MEDIUM (oversupplied), and aerospace ~ZERO — matching every known-truth case.
