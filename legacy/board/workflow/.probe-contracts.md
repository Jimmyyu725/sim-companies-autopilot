(confirmed via `cdp.capture`); clicking the INCOMING/OUTGOING tabs fires **no** additional fetch —
the tabs render the page-load data. So a poller can just GET `contracts-incoming/0/me/` directly.

The accept/decline/send **mutation** endpoints were NOT observed (nothing was clicked/sent). Per
sell-exchange-ui.js precedent, assume mutations require the app's signed headers → must go through
the UI, not raw POST. (Guess, unverified: they live under `/api/v2/contracts.../<id>/` — do not
code against this.)

## C. Send-contract flow (verified dry on the water tile, warehouse)

Flow has an extra step v1 missed — a **quality-lot selection** between the opener and the form:

1. `goto /headquarters/warehouse/` → **real-mouse click** the resource tile
   (img src matches `/water\.[0-9a-f]*\.png/` — tile img filenames are content-hashed, e.g.
   `water.8df7bb28fb93.png`; React ignores synthetic clicks on tiles, same as sell-exchange-ui.js).
2. Tile detail panel buttons: `SELL ON THE EXCHANGE` / `SEND CONTRACT` / `PUT ON DISPLAY`
   (all `btn btn-secondary`, class `css-so57tv`).
3. Click `SEND CONTRACT` (the **opener** — safe, it only switches the panel) → panel shows
   `SELECT QUALITY ABOVE`: you must click one of the quality/sourcing lot rows, e.g.
   `2,063 $0.376(100.00%)` / `68 $0.383(100.00%)` (innermost div matching
   `/^\d[\d,]*\s+\$[\d.]+\s*\(\d+\.\d+%\)/`). **Real-mouse click** the row (selection, not a confirm).
4. The contract form then renders **inline in the panel** (NOT a modal — `div[role=dialog]` absent):
   - `input[name=recipientLookup]` type=text, placeholder `Company` — typing fires the
     autocomplete GET `/api/v2/companies/list/0/<query>/`.
   - `input[name=amount]` type=number (hint text `Please enter amount`), plus an `ALL` button
     (`btn btn-secondary`) that fills the full lot quantity.
   - `input[name=price]` type=number — PRICE PER UNIT.
   - **`SEND CONTRACT` — `btn btn-primary` (class `css-1pdlpeu`), starts `disabled` until the form
     validates. THIS is the final confirm. Never click without confirm:true.**
   - `Cancel` — `link-button link-color` — closes the form cleanly (preferred dry-close; Escape
     also works, and navigating away clears it).

**Ambiguity trap for the future primitive:** opener and final confirm share the exact label
`SEND CONTRACT`. Disambiguate like scrap.js: keep the opener's element identity and exclude it
(`b !== openerBtn`), and/or require `btn-primary` + it being the one that flips from disabled to
enabled after the fields are filled. If not exactly one candidate → refuse.
