# Probe: features the brain does NOT have (encyclopedia + bundle archaeology)

Date: 2026-07-24. Sources: `encyclopedia/*.txt` (scraped in-game), `game-facts.json` (measured),
route table + i18n strings mined from `bundle-main.js` (offsets ~671k route map, ~2.1M–2.5M message

Brain today: produce / sell(retail) / buy / build / upgrade / scrap / bonds / exchange_sell /
pa_read / pa_reply / collect + **read-only** contracts. Everything below is what a human player
also uses and we lack.

Level gates (measured, `misc-levels.txt` + game-facts): Lv5 Contracts + Seasonal, Lv10 Bonds +

## TIER 1 — usable at Lv.11 TODAY

### 1. Research → Quality (patents)  [unlocked at Lv.10 — we have it and don't use it]
- What: buy/produce research units (labs: Plant research center, Kitchen, etc.), then **Apply
  research** on a resource. Each application rolls for patents (base probability 6.25%, measured);
  patents accumulate toward the next quality level: 12, 50, 500, 2000, 5000... per Q step.
- Why: quality gives a **retail price bonus** ("{percentage}% quality bonus") and **execution
  priority on the exchange** (bundle: "higher quality ... better margins in retail and increase
  execution priority on the exchange"). Q1 on apples/oranges/grapes is cheap (12 patents) and
  permanently raises retail margin.
- API: `GET/POST /api/v3/players/research/`; UI strings under `Research.*`.
- Priority: **HIGH** — cheapest permanent margin upgrade available to us right now.

### 2. Seasonal markets  [unlocked at Lv.5; Summer season is ACTIVE Jul 08 – Aug 29]
- What: during a retail season the **city gives you the seasonal market building for FREE** — it
  "cannot be upgraded. It doesn't contribute to the administration overhead, company building
  count, or company value" (bundle string). Beach market (letter `z`, wage $241/h) retails
  CHOCOLATE ICECREAM and APPLE ICECREAM at **"Demand: very high"**.
- Why for us: we grow APPLES; APPLE ICECREAM is made in a Food processing plant (inputs incl.
  apples/milk per resource pages 101/112/123/134). Even just buying icecream on the exchange and
  retailing it through the free market is a seasonal margin play. Time-boxed: ends Aug 29.
  Other seasons: Ramadan (Feb 18–Mar 19), Easter/Spring market (Apr), Halloween market (Oct),
  Xmas market (Dec) — all with their own very-high-demand goods.
- Priority: **HIGH (time-critical)** — a free retail slot we are currently wasting.

### 3. Contracts — the ACTIVE side (we are read-only)  [Lv.5]
- What: accept/decline incoming contract offers (`PATCH/POST /api/v2/contracts/{id}/`), send
  outgoing offers from the market page (frontend route `:resourceName/contract`). Direct
  company-to-company recurring trades at negotiated prices; sellers (e.g. SPC Trading) supply
  high-Q inputs by contract. Contracts consume transportation units like retail.
- Why: locks in input costs below exchange ask and sells surplus without waiting on the order
  book; also the door to Q-input supply once we care about quality.
- Priority: **HIGH.**

### 4. Exchange order management: cancel / reprice + take
- What we lack: `Exchange.cancelOrder` — cancelling a posted sell order (charged the unpaid rest
  of the 4% fee, or **free after a date** shown in the dialog); repricing stale listings;
  `POST /api/v2/order/take/{id}/` (take a specific listed order). Later at Lv.25: **limit buy
  orders** ("Post a buy order... stays on the exchange until matched; cash refunded in full on
  cancel") — `/api/v3/market/buy/`, `/api/v2/companies/{id}/market-buy-orders/`.
- Why: exchange_sell without cancel/reprice means stale orders sit undercut for days.
- Priority: **HIGH** (cancel/reprice now; buy orders are a Lv.25 note).

### 5. Production-modifier intel (market events, weather, sales modifier)
- What: `GET /api/v2/production-modifiers/{id}/`, `GET /api/v2/weather/{id}/`; encyclopedia
  events page shows ±20–30% production events with dates (e.g. Coffee beans +21% since 07/12,
  Salad −28%, Planks −26%...). Client state also carries `productionModifier` / `salesModifier`.
- Why: choosing WHAT to produce while ignoring a live +21% on coffee beans leaves EV on the
  table; pure read, zero risk.
- Priority: **MEDIUM-HIGH** (read-only feed into produce decisions).

### 6. SimBoost economy + rush
- What: SimBoosts = secondary currency earned free (achievements, veteran rewards, welcome-back
  bonus, contests) and purchasable. Spend actions enumerated in bundle: Construction Speedup,
  **Exchange for Cash**, Executive Unlock/Training/Hiring/Settling/Strike-resolution, Building
  Slot Unlock, Building Auction slot/promote, Production-or-Sale **Bonus Realign**, Display Case
  Unlock, Robots Installation Speedup, etc. `POST /api/v1/rush/{id}/` finishes a running job.
- Why: free boosts accrue and we never spend them; "Exchange for Cash" is a cash valve, rush can
  compress build times on upgrades.
- Priority: **MEDIUM** (inventory what we have first via `/api/v2/players/simboosts-use/`).

### 7. Achievements / objectives / redeem codes / referrals
- What: achievements pay one-time rewards (and feed certificates); the header objectives ("Invest
  into infrastructure / Build new farm") are guided suggestions; `/redeem-bonus-code/`,
  `/api/v2/players/bonuses/`, referrals.
- Priority: **LOW-MEDIUM** — occasional free money; worth a one-time sweep, not a loop.

## TIER 2 — next unlocks (prepare, can't use yet)

### 8. Executives + Academy + poaching  [Lv.15 — our nearest big unlock]
- What: hire a C-suite (skills measured in bundle: **COO=Management → production, CFO=Accounting
  → cuts accounting/admin fees, CMO=Communication → retail/sales, CTO=Science → patent
  probability + research production speed**; also restaurant rating). Hire via agencies
  (Top talent / Good / Staffing / In-house graduate), pay **daily salaries** (strike after 1 unpaid
  day, leave after 2), train at the Academy building (trainings o/f/m/t/g), retire/dismiss with
  severance. Apprentice/Staff slots; diminishing returns above a skill cutoff.
- **Poaching** (the `fromPoaching` cashflow line): other companies send **hostile offers** for your
  execs (`/api/v3/companies/executives/hostile-offers/`); you can counter-offer, research the
  poacher, or let go. Poaching COSTS = "agency fees, sign-on bonuses, and training reimbursements";
  poaching INCOME = "cash from executive training reimbursements" when your trained exec is taken.
  Former execs elsewhere generate **royalties** (`/api/v2/companies/{id}/royalties/`).
- APIs: `/api/v3/companies/{id}/executives/`, `/api/v4/executives/{id}/`, `.../trainings/`,
  `/api/v2/companies/executives/my-offers/`.
- Priority: **HIGH at Lv.15** (esp. CFO vs accounting overhead and CTO vs patent grind). Budget
  daily salaries before hiring — strikes hurt.

### 9. Government orders  [Lv.20]
- What: weekly batches of big fulfillment orders; companies are assigned a **Tier**; you create or
  join a multi-company **Bid** (deposit required, invite-only or public), bidding deadline, then
  awarded contractors deliver "per unit of {resource} Q{quality}" for a fixed compensation.
  "Your company is too small to participate" until tiered. Whole API family under
  `/api/v3/government-orders/...` (realm bids, contractors, company bids, tier).
- Priority: **MEDIUM at Lv.20** — solid guaranteed-price demand for bulk producers.

### 10. Building auctions  [Lv.20]
- What: auction real buildings between players (`/market/building-auctions/`,
  `/api/v2/building-auctions/...` incl. bids, promote, research-by-auction). One active auction
  free, more cost simboosts. Certificates: Real Estate Mogul (most sold), Wallet Wielder (highest
  value bought). Selling a leveled building can beat scrap (scrap returns materials at Q0).
- Priority: **MEDIUM at Lv.20.**

### 11. Buy orders  [Lv.25] — see #4; standing limit-buys with minQ filter.

## TIER 3 — situational / other verticals

### 12. Industrial robots
- What: install robots in production buildings — "Increase production and sales speed by
  {percent}%" and "reduce workforce by {percent}%" (wage cut); robots consumed per level
  (e.g. Vertical integration: 40×Q0 at L1 → 360×Q2 at L9); can't upgrade while installed;
  uninstall returns robots at Q0.
- Priority: LOW now (capital-heavy), MEDIUM once buildings are L5+.

### 13. Abundance (Mine / Quarry / Oil rig)
- What: extraction buildings roll a hidden **abundance %** per resource (Poor/Low/Good/Excellent)
  that governs output; UI warns "low abundance ... should be scrapped. Try building it again for
  a chance at better abundance" — scrap-and-reroll loop. `/api/v2/companies/buildings/{id}/abundance/`.
- Priority: N/A unless we enter extraction.

### 14. Restaurant / Catering vertical
- What: Restaurant (letter `r`) runs "restaurant runs" with staff quality and public **ratings**
  (Simicheline star certificate); own endpoints `restaurant-properties/`, `restaurant-runs/`;
  reconstruction cycles. A completely separate retail engine from stores.
- Priority: LOW (different business model; revisit much later).

### 15. Sales offices (letter `B`) + aerospace endgame
- What: the retail outlet for aerospace goods — Jumbo/Luxury jet, single engine plane, satellite
  (demand mostly "low"). Downstream of Hangar/Aerospace factory/Launch pad; rocket launches
  (`/api/v3/rocket-launches/`) can explode (Elon award). There is also a Launch pad, Bank
  (boosts executive accounting effectiveness), and Academy (exec training) in the sidebar that
  were never scraped as pages.
- Priority: N/A for apple.co.

### 16. Contests + resource donation
- What: live contest in every scrape header: "The Great SimCity Space Race". Donate the contest
  resource (`/api/v2/resources/{id}/donate/`); first to deliver wins, top 5 get certificates,
  winner + recipients get XP; checkpoints every 24h.
- Priority: LOW — resource sink; only if a contest matches what we already overproduce.

### 17. PUT ON DISPLAY  (the question asked)
- Two meanings, both **cosmetic, zero economic effect**:
  a) `Achievements.putOnDisplay` — pin an achievement on your public company profile;
  b) Collectibles **display case** (`/api/v2/companies/{id}/display-case/`, "Do you want to put
     up {what} on display for everyone to see?") — showcase collectibles; the case itself is a
     simboost unlock.
- Priority: **NONE.** Safely ignore.

### 18. Collectibles / NFT vault
- What: rare numbered drops (e.g. "Aluminium Q10 (#3)") land in the Collectibles Vault; trade on
  the collectibles market (`/market/collectibles/`, trades priced in simboosts). Pure windfall —
  if a drop ever appears, sell it; no loop needed.

### 19. Social & misc (all LOW/none)
- Gift baskets (simboost gifting), Egg collecting (Easter-only minigame), Newspaper articles +
  ads/sponsorship (marketing/social), polls, chatrooms + AI chat summaries, company free-text/
  tags/notes, contacts, company ratings (AAA..D affect OUR bond pricing — read-only relevant to
  the bonds module), realms (separate worlds; Magnates = phase 8 per game-facts), reposition
  (moving buildings on the landscape map — purely visual), CSV report exports
  (`/csv/balance-sheet/` etc. — could be handy for accounting.js), HQ skins (Obsidian/Cyber
  Tower = paid cosmetics), "administration overhead / plus-one" endpoint pair (accounting
  overhead scales with cash + buildings; the **Bank** building + CFO skill reduce it — becomes
  interesting the richer we get).
