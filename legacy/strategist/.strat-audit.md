its spend is healthy; the dilution comes from the newly-added **beef line**: Cows $2,361 + Steak
$2,095 = **$4,456 of slaughterhouse inputs** now blended into the sales mix.

**Most likely cause (INFERENCE, not measured per-product this run): product-mix dilution during a
line ramp.** The extra sales volume is padded with more low-margin steak/cow output plus freshly-online
coffee-powder input cost, so each sales dollar now costs more to make than the powder-heavy 07-23 mix.
`cashToExchange` deepened to −$57,108 (more inputs bought) and wages rose to −$13,477 (more production

Note: **net income is flat and misleading** ($26,699 → $26,412, −$287). It only holds up because one-off
