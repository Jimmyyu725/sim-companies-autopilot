COMPANY INVESTMENT RATING

The ratings are assigned to each company by Sim Companies own rating agency which does not disclose the exact criteria. It takes into account if the company is managed daily, how much value is in the assets, bonds, current bond liabilities, and cashflow. It also looks at the current level, certificates the company got and if interest payments were paid in the past.

The presented ratings are strictly an opinion of the agency. The agency is not responsible for any financial loss incurred due to making a bad investment based on the provided ratings.

In case of default, a restructured percentage of the bond face value is recovered by the bond holder.
